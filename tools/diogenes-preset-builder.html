A preset holds what varies with what you are doing. Choose, watch what it does to the windows, take the file. Dictionary paths are not here on purpose: those are facts about the machine and belong in your init file, where they stay put whichever preset you load.

A direction asks Emacs nothing. Let Emacs choose goes through split-window-sensibly, which consults split-height-threshold — and a distribution may set that so that no frame you have can be split.
